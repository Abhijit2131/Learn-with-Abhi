Unit Wise Syllabus

Unit Course Contents Hours
Unit 1 DC Electric Circuit, Electrical & Electronics, Current, Voltage, Resistor, Capacitor, Inductor, Ohm's Law, Waveforms & Signals, KCL, KVL, Series-Parallel Circuit, Star-Delta Transformation and Numericals. 08
Unit 2 DC Motor: Principle, Construction, Working, Types and Speed-Torque Characteristics. Single Phase Transformer: Principle, Construction, Classification, Voltage Ratio, Current Ratio, Losses & Efficiency. 08
Unit 3 Semiconductor Basics, PN Junction Diode, LED, Zener Diode, Rectifiers, Half Wave, Full Wave, Bridge Rectifier and Comparison. 07
Unit 4 Transistor Introduction, BJT Construction, Working, Operating Regions, Configurations and Applications. 07
Unit 5 Analog vs Digital Electronics, Number System, Binary Arithmetic, 1's & 2's Complement, Logic Gates, Universal and Derived Gates. 07
Unit 6 8085 Microprocessor, Architecture, ALU, Registers, Bus System, Pin Diagram, Addressing Modes, Instruction Set and Applications. 08

Course Outcomes (COs)

CO1

Identify electrical quantities, circuit elements and waveform parameters.

CO2

Describe construction and working of DC Motors and Single Phase Transformers.

CO3

Interpret the operation and applications of Diodes.

CO4

Illustrate transistor operation and practical applications.

CO5

Apply number systems and logic gate concepts in digital electronics.

CO6

Describe the architecture and functional blocks of the 8085 Microprocessor.

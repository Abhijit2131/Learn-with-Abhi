1. Introduction

Electrical and Electronics Engineering is a branch of engineering concerned with the generation, transmission, control and utilization of electrical energy along with the design and application of electronic devices for communication, automation and digital systems.

10. Ohm's Law

Statement: At constant temperature, the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the applied voltage and inversely proportional to its resistance.
V = I × R

Ohm's Law Triangle

Electrical Circuit

Worked Numerical

GivenValue
Voltage12 V
Resistance4 Ω
Current?

Solution

V = I × R

I = V / R

I = 12 / 4

I = 3 A
Answer: Current flowing through the circuit = 3 Ampere

11. Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL)

Statement: The algebraic sum of all currents entering and leaving a node is always zero.
ΣI = 0

Kirchhoff's Current Law is based on the Law of Conservation of Charge. It states that electric charge cannot accumulate at a junction.

Node Diagram

Mathematical Form

I₁ + I₂ = I₃ + I₄

Solved Numerical

GivenValue
I₁4 A
I₂2 A
I₃3 A
I₄?

Solution

I₁ + I₂ = I₃ + I₄

4 + 2 = 3 + I₄

I₄ = 3 A
Answer: Current leaving the junction = 3 A

12. Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL)

Statement: The algebraic sum of all voltages around any closed loop is always zero.
ΣV = 0

KVL is based on the Law of Conservation of Energy. The total voltage supplied equals the total voltage consumed.

Closed Loop Circuit

Voltage Equation

+10 − 4 − 6 = 0

Solved Numerical

Supply10 V
R₁ Drop4 V
R₂ Drop6 V

Verification

ΣV = 0

10 - 4 - 6

= 0 ✔

13. Series & Parallel Circuits

Series Circuit

In a series circuit, components are connected one after another. The same current flows through every resistor.

R = R₁ + R₂ + R₃

Parallel Circuit

In a parallel circuit, voltage across each branch remains the same while current divides among the branches.

1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃

Comparison

SeriesParallel
Current is sameVoltage is same
Voltage dividesCurrent divides
R increasesR decreases

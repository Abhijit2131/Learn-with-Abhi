1.1 Definition of Electronics

Electronics is one of the most important branches of engineering that deals with the behaviour and control of electrons in electronic devices and circuits.

Definition (IRE):

"According to the Institute of Radio Engineering (IRE), Electronics is a branch of Science and Engineering which deals with the study of electronic devices, their processing and utilization."

Simple Meaning:
Electronics is the branch of engineering that studies devices such as Diodes, Transistors, ICs, Sensors and Microprocessors used for processing, controlling and transmitting electrical signals.

1.2 Applications of Electronics

Electronics plays a crucial role across many sectors including defense, industrial automation, healthcare, telecommunication and domestic appliances. Modern life is highly dependent on electronic systems for communication, automation and intelligent control.

1. Defense

Radar systems, missile guidance, military drones, secure satellite communication, sonar and night vision equipment strengthen national security.

2. Industrial & Instrumentation

PLCs, industrial robots, inverters, oscilloscopes, multimeters, flow meters and pressure sensors are used for automation and process control.

3. Medical

MRI, CT Scanner, ECG machine, Pacemaker, Ultrasound and Pulse Oximeter help doctors in diagnosis and patient monitoring.

4. Telecommunication

Smartphones, satellite communication, fiber optic networks, internet infrastructure and wireless communication connect the world.

5. Domestic Applications

Smart TVs, refrigerators, washing machines, microwave ovens, smart lighting, laptops and wearable devices make everyday life easier.

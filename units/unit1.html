1. Introduction

Electrical and Electronics Engineering is a branch of engineering concerned with the generation, transmission, control and utilization of electrical energy along with the design and application of electronic devices for communication, automation and digital systems.

Course Outcome Addressed: CO1 – Identify basic electrical quantities, circuit elements and waveform parameters used in electrical and electronic circuits.

2. Difference Between Electrical & Electronics Engineering

Electrical Engineering Electronics Engineering
Deals with high power systems Deals with low power systems
Mainly uses AC supply Mainly uses DC supply
Power generation & distribution Signal processing & communication
Machines and transformers Diodes, transistors & ICs
kW to MW range mW to W range

10. Ohm's Law

Statement: At constant temperature, the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the applied voltage and inversely proportional to its resistance.
V = I × R

Ohm's Law Triangle

Electrical Circuit

Worked Numerical

GivenValue
Voltage12 V
Resistance4 Ω
Current?

Solution

V = I × R

I = V / R

I = 12 / 4

I = 3 A
Answer: Current flowing through the circuit = 3 Ampere

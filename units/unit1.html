1. Introduction

Electrical and Electronics Engineering deals with generation, control, transmission and utilization of electrical energy along with electronic devices used for communication, automation and digital systems.

Learning Outcome: Identify basic electrical quantities, passive elements and waveform parameters used in electrical and electronic circuits.

2. Difference Between Electrical & Electronics Engineering

Electrical Engineering Electronics Engineering
Deals with high power systems Deals with low power systems
Mainly uses AC supply Mainly uses DC supply
Power generation and transmission Signal processing and communication
Machines and transformers Diodes, transistors and ICs
Voltage range is high Voltage range is comparatively low

9. Ohm's Law

At constant temperature, the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the applied voltage and inversely proportional to its resistance.

Formula

V = I × R

Simple Circuit

Worked Example

Voltage (V)12 V
Resistance (R)4 Ω
Current (I)?
**Solution** 
I = V/R

I = 12/4

I = 3 Ampere
Answer: The current flowing through the circuit is 3 A.
